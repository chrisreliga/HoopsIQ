The Breakdown

Three things that decide this season

The Books what the money says

Being over the cap isn't unusual — most contenders are. Being over the second apron is different. It's the line the league drew to stop teams buying their way out of mistakes. They can't sign anybody good, they can't merge two salaries into one bigger one in a trade, and a future first-rounder gets frozen as a penalty.

  • No mid-level
  • No sign-and-trade
  • 2033 pick frozen

The Core what the talent says

Booker, Green and Brooks have never won a playoff series together, and last spring they got swept. The scoring is real — 9th in offense — but the shot quality tells on them. The encouraging part is underneath: four players 24 or younger on deals totalling less than Green makes by himself.

  • 9th in offense
  • 22nd in defense
  • Swept in round 1

The Test what has to happen

One playoff series. Not a title, not a conference finals — one series won with Booker as the best player on the floor. Win it and the supermax looks like continuity instead of a trap. Lose it and the clock on Green's option becomes the loudest thing in the building.

  • Win 1 series
  • Green option · 2028

First real relief

Summer 2028

$34.4M comes off the books when Green's option resolves. That's the first offseason Phoenix can meaningfully change anything.
